# Official Sharma logo — November 2026

Sanjay made this the official logo on 22 Nov 2026.

## Files

- `logo-color.svg` — color lockup: navy `#264387` wordmark, lion, and tagline; gold `#F0A846` bolt and breath; transparent background. Use on white.
- `logo-white.svg` — white wordmark, lion, and tagline; gold accents; transparent background. Use on navy `#264387` or dark navy `#1a2f61` headers, and other dark backgrounds.

Print PNG and PDF of the same art belong in this folder as `logo-color.png` / `.pdf` and `logo-white.png` / `.pdf` when added.

## Rules

- Use the SVG on screens.
- White on navy `#264387` / dark navy `#1a2f61` headers; color on white.
- Minimum width 160px for the full lockup with tagline.
- Keep the art as-is. Do not redraw, recolor, or rearrange it.
- Tagline is **POWER – COMFORT – TRUST** with dashes, never periods.
- This replaces the old logo PNGs everywhere: headers, lock screens, PWA icons, favicons, email signatures, and quote/invoice docs.
- Sharma Track keeps its own brand.

Do not change anything live until Mark signs off on screenshots.
